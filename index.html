Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-08

Overview

Percentage Solver does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. All calculation history is stored locally on your device.

Calculation History

If you enable calculation history in Settings, your recent calculations are saved locally on your device only. You can clear all history at any time from the Settings screen. This data is never sent to any server.

Advertising

Percentage Solver uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect and use data including your device's Advertising ID to show personalised ads. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used is Google AdMob. We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or any other third-party SDK.

Children

Percentage Solver is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect data from children.
